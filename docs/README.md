## Ground rules used across these documents

- Coverage is **Kuala Lumpur and Selangor only**, as general areas, never a guarantee for every
  neighbourhood.
- **Installation (corrected 2026-10-20):** the owner has confirmed that AJ **does** install air
  conditioners. The website FAQ now says so. The company profile PDF still says installation is
  _not_ offered, so that document is out of date and needs updating. Until the owner confirms the
  scope (which unit types), pricing and any brands, do not publish installation prices, packages,
  inclusions or specifications anywhere.
- No invented claims: no ratings, awards, certifications, guarantees, response-time promises,
  years in business, or "best/cheapest" wording.
- No guaranteed rankings, reach, leads or sales.
- The registered address on the SSM certificate is a residential unit and must not be published.
