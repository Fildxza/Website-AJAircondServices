# Google Business Profile: setup guide for the owner

**Who does this:** the business owner, signed in with their own Google account. Nobody else
should create, claim, verify or edit the listing on the owner's behalf, and no one (including
the marketing operator or an AI assistant) has done so. This guide only explains the steps.

Google changes its screens and rules from time to time. Where a rule matters, the official page
is linked. Check it on the day you do the work.

- Sign up: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/10514137>
- Service-area businesses: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177>

## Why AJ should set this up

AJ travels to customers' homes and premises, so it is a **service-area business**: it serves
customers at their locations rather than at a shop. Google allows one service-area profile for a
business like this. A complete, accurate profile is one of the ways local customers can find AJ
on Google Search and Maps. It does not guarantee any ranking.

## Before you start (have these ready)

- A Google account that the **owner** controls and will keep (not a personal account of an employee
  or the marketing operator).
- The exact business name: **AJ Airconditioning Services**.
- The verified phone numbers: +60 18-283 3900 and +60 18-262 1417.
- The production website URL, **once confirmed** (see `deployment-notes.md`). Leave the website
  field empty until then rather than entering a temporary or wrong address.
- A real address for **verification only** (the registered business address). It must stay hidden
  from customers, see step 5.

## Setup steps

1. **Sign in** to the owner's Google account and go to <https://business.google.com/create>.
2. **Business name.** Enter `AJ Airconditioning Services` exactly as it is used on signage,
   invoices and the website. Do not add keywords or locations to the name (for example
   "…Best Aircond KL"). Google can suspend profiles for that.
3. **Category.** Choose the most specific primary category Google offers that describes an
   air-conditioning service or repair business. Add secondary categories only if they are true.
   Pick from Google's current list as you type; the names change.
4. **Customer visits.** When asked whether customers can visit a location, answer **No**, because
   AJ serves customers at their own locations.
5. **Hide the address.** Google's guidance for service-area businesses is that the address should
   be hidden from customers. The address you give is for verification only. Never use a PO box,
   mailbox or virtual office. Note: the registered SSM address is a residential unit; keeping it
   hidden protects the owner's privacy.
6. **Service area.** Add the areas AJ really serves: **Kuala Lumpur** and **Selangor**. Google
   allows up to 20 areas and advises that the area should not extend much beyond about two hours'
   driving time from where the business is based. Do **not** add neighbourhoods unless the owner has
   verified them (see the decision register in `README.md`).
7. **Contact details.** Enter the phone number the owner wants customers to use. Add the website
   URL only once it is confirmed, using the format in `utm-links.md`.
8. **Verification.** Choose "Verify now" and follow the method Google offers for this business.
   Google decides which methods are available, so do not buy or pay anyone for "guaranteed
   verification". Keep the verification code or message private.
9. **Business hours.** Enter hours only if they are real and will be kept. If AJ works by
   appointment, say so in the description instead of inventing hours.
10. **Services.** Add the confirmed services: General servicing, Chemical cleaning, Gas top-up
    (refrigerant gas services), Repair and troubleshooting, Preventive maintenance. The owner has
    since confirmed that AJ also **installs** air conditioners, so "Installation" can be added as
    a service once the owner confirms its scope. Add prices only after the pricing scope is
    confirmed (decision register items 3 and 12).
11. **Description** (draft to review, based only on verified facts):

    > AJ Airconditioning Services provides aircond servicing, chemical cleaning, gas top-up,
    > troubleshooting, repair and preventive maintenance for homes and business premises in
    > Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. We also install air conditioners. WhatsApp us to check if we
    > cover your area.

12. **Photos and logo.** Upload only photos the owner has approved, with staff, customer and
    third-party-brand consent. Use the real logo only; a square vector or high-resolution version
    from the designer is better than the small crop currently available. Never upload stock or
    generated pictures presented as AJ's work.

## Reviews (honestly earned only)

- Asking customers for a review is allowed. **Offering anything in return is not** (no discounts,
  gifts, prizes or favours), and you must not ask only the customers you expect to be happy.
- Do not write reviews for AJ, ask staff or relatives to, or buy reviews. Google can remove them,
  warn customers and restrict the profile.
- Practical routine: after **every** completed job, send the customer the profile's review link by
  WhatsApp with a short thank-you. Reply politely to every review, good or bad, and never argue.
- Do not promise a rating. Reviews should describe real experiences.

## Giving the marketing operator access (optional)

Use the profile's **people and access** settings to invite the operator's own Google account with
the lowest role that lets them do the job. The owner stays the primary owner. Never share the
owner's Google password. Remove the operator's access when the engagement ends. Record the date
access was granted and what it covers.

## Keeping the profile healthy

- Check calls, messages and reviews weekly and reply promptly.
- Keep name, phone, service area, services and website identical to the website.
- Update the profile whenever the owner confirms something new (for example a verified area or
  pricing scope). Do not edit it to show claims the website does not make.
- Do not create a second profile for the same business in the same city. Google may suspend the
  account.

## What the owner must do personally (cannot be delegated)

- Create or claim the profile and complete verification.
- Choose and confirm the category, service area, hours, services and description.
- Decide what appears about pricing.
- Approve photos and the logo.
- Grant and remove other people's access.
