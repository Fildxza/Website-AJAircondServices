# 90-day growth roadmap

A practical plan to turn the new website into a steady flow of local enquiries in Kuala Lumpur
and Selangor. It is a plan, not a promise: **no ranking, reach, lead count or sales figure is
guaranteed**, and none is forecast here. Targets are set from AJ's own numbers at Day 30.

## Ground rules (apply to every task below)

- Serve and advertise **Kuala Lumpur and Selangor only**, by general area. No neighbourhood claims
  until verified by the owner.
- AJ **does** install air conditioners (owner's correction, 2026-10-20). Say only that it is
  offered. Do not publish installation prices, packages, brands or timeframes until the owner
  confirms them.
- Say only what is true and approved: no ratings, awards, certifications, guarantees, "best",
  "cheapest", response-time promises or years in business. ("Since 2023" is on hold.)
- Prices: use only the approved RM130 / RM230 / RM230 by unit type, and only with the wording the
  owner has confirmed (pricing scope is still open).
- **Photos and video of real jobs need consent** from staff, customers and for any visible
  third-party branding. No stock or generated images presented as AJ's work.
- Reviews must be real and unrewarded. Never buy, write or gate them.
- Do not name clients from the company profile without each client's permission.
- Log every enquiry in the lead sheet from day one ([lead-tracking-spec.md](lead-tracking-spec.md)).

## Roles

- **Owner (AJ):** approvals, job photos and consent, answering enquiries, review requests, revenue entries.
- **Marketing operator:** drafting content, posting, community outreach, reporting. Works only
  from approved facts.
- Agree who replies on WhatsApp, and when, **before** anything is promoted. Fast, friendly replies
  decide whether a lead becomes a booking.

## Before Day 1: readiness gate

Do not start promoting until these are done. Each takes little time but avoids wasted effort.

- [ ] Production URL confirmed and the site live ([deployment-notes.md](deployment-notes.md))
- [ ] Lead sheet created and tested with 2–3 practice rows; one person owns it
- [ ] WhatsApp reply routine agreed (who, which hours, template replies)
- [ ] Google Business Profile started by the owner ([google-business-profile-guide.md](google-business-profile-guide.md))
- [ ] Owner has answered or explicitly deferred the decision register ([README.md](README.md))
- [ ] List of approved facts for content: services, the two areas, unit types, approved prices
- [ ] Photo and video consent routine in place (a simple "OK to use this photo?" message, saved)
- [ ] UTM naming agreed ([utm-links.md](utm-links.md))

## Content pillars (reuse everywhere)

1. **Explain it.** Short, honest, educational videos and posts about aircond care.
2. **Show the work.** Real, consented before-and-after and process clips.
3. **Make it easy to book.** Clear path to WhatsApp, who AJ serves, what AJ does not do.
4. **Prove it.** Real reviews and thank-you replies, with the customer's permission where they are shown.
5. **Be local.** Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, in plain language. Do not list neighbourhoods unverified.

Starter ideas (check every claim against what is true for AJ before publishing):

- What happens during a chemical clean (steps AJ really follows)
- Signs your aircond may need servicing (weak airflow, water leaking, odour, noise, not cooling)
- "My aircond isn't cold. Is it gas?" Explain that low refrigerant is one possible cause among others,
  as the FAQ already says
- Wall mounted vs ceiling cassette vs ceiling exposed units: what each looks like
- Why AJ prices by unit type rather than horsepower
- What a technician checks after servicing (testing the cooling with you)
- "Do you cover my area?" Explain how to check on WhatsApp
- "Do you install air conditioners?" A short, honest answer (yes), once the owner has confirmed
  which unit types and how enquiries are handled
- Behind the scenes (only with staff consent)

Format guidance: short vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels (one idea per
video, a spoken hook in the first seconds, captions on screen for viewers who watch muted), plus
plain photo posts. Use Bahasa Malaysia and English as suits the audience. Every post ends with
one clear action (WhatsApp or the website link, using a UTM link).

## Days 1–30: foundation and first proof

**Week 1: set up**

- Launch the site and confirm every button works on a real phone.
- Complete the Business Profile (owner), with accurate name, phone, service area, services and description.
- Create or tidy the Facebook page and Instagram and TikTok accounts with the real logo, the same
  name and the website link (UTM).
- Start the lead sheet. Log every enquiry from here on.

**Week 2–3: first content**

- Publish a few posts per week (a sensible starting point; adjust to what the team can keep up).
  Aim for consistency over volume.
- Film 6–8 short educational videos in one batch (no customer or staff on camera unless consented).
- Ask the owner to approve any real job photos and note the consent.
- Share the website on personal and business WhatsApp status and in relevant community groups
  **where allowed by the group rules**.

**Week 4: first review loop**

- After every completed job, send the review link with a thank-you. Never offer anything for a review.
- Reply to every review and every comment.
- **Day-30 review (30 minutes):** read the lead sheet and the Business Profile insights. Record
  baselines: valid leads per source, replies within the agreed time, bookings, completed jobs.
  Choose realistic targets for days 31–90 from these baselines.

## Days 31–60: consistency and local presence

- Keep the posting rhythm. Re-use the best-performing idea in a new format (a video becomes a photo
  post, a caption, a status).
- **Community outreach** (the main non-digital channel):
  - Residents' associations, joint management bodies and property managers of condominiums and apartment blocks
  - Offices, shops, cafes, clinics and other business premises in KL and Selangor, using the QR
    notice-board link in `utm-links.md`
  - Neighbours, friends and existing customers (ask them to share AJ's details)
  - Introduce AJ, what it does (including installation, once its scope is confirmed) and the two
    areas covered. Leave a
    flyer or card. Do not claim any client relationship you have not been given permission to mention.
- If the owner wants, ask existing customers for **referrals**. Any referral thank-you must be
  separate from reviews, since nothing may be offered for a review.
- Improve the website from real questions: add or reword FAQ entries when the same question arrives repeatedly.
- **Day-60 review:** compare with Day 30. Which sources bring valid leads that become bookings? Stop or
  change what is not working. Keep what is.

## Days 61–90: measure, refine, decide on paid

- Double down on the two or three activities with the best lead-to-booking results in the lead sheet.
- Collect and (with permission) feature genuine customer comments. Never edit or invent them.
- Review the lead sheet for patterns: unit types requested, most common area, "Not a fit"
  enquiries (a high number means the message about coverage or services needs to be clearer).
- **Paid advertising is optional and must clear this gate first:**
  1. The lead sheet has been used consistently for at least 30 days
  2. AJ can answer new enquiries quickly and has spare job capacity
  3. The site is live on its confirmed URL
  4. The owner has set a budget **and a stop rule** (for example, review after a fixed spend)
  5. Ad text and images are approved and truthful. Any installation wording matches what the owner
     has confirmed, with no invented prices or specifications
  6. The owner has reviewed privacy and consent for any ad pixel or analytics tool (not built yet)
     Start with a small test, measure cost per **valid lead** from the sheet (not clicks), and stop if
     it does not work. Ad platform rules and formats change, so check the platform's current policies.
- **Day-90 review:** decide with the owner what to continue, stop, or test next. Decide whether to
  add analytics and event tracking (needs approval, see `utm-links.md`).

## Weekly rhythm (about an hour for the operator, 15 minutes for the owner)

| When     | Task                                                                     |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Monday   | Plan the week's posts from the content pillars                           |
| Mid-week | Publish; reply to comments and messages                                  |
| Friday   | Update the lead sheet with the owner; reply to reviews; note what worked |
| Monthly  | Written report (below); owner and operator review together               |

## Scorecard (set targets at Day 30, never before)

| Measure                                           | Where it comes from                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Valid enquiries, by source                        | Lead sheet, `Monthly`                    |
| Time to first reply                               | Lead sheet                               |
| Enquiries that became bookings and completed jobs | Lead sheet                               |
| Revenue (only what the owner shares)              | Lead sheet, owner entry                  |
| New genuine reviews and average rating            | Business Profile (shown, not controlled) |
| Posts published and video views                   | Each platform (supporting figures only)  |
| "Not a fit" enquiries                             | Lead sheet (a clarity check)             |

Followers, likes and views are useful signals but are **not** results. Judge the plan by valid
leads, bookings and completed jobs.

## Monthly report (one page)

1. What was published and what was done in the community
2. Valid leads by source, and the Unknown share
3. Bookings and completed jobs
4. What worked and what did not, in plain words
5. Next month's plan and any decision needed from the owner

## Attribution and commission

Leads are credited to marketing only on the evidence rules in the lead-sheet document: what the
customer says, or a campaign-specific contact. UTM clicks, traffic and timing alone do not credit
anyone. Any commission arrangement is agreed in writing between the owner and the operator.

## What not to do

- Do not buy followers, reviews or "guaranteed ranking" services.
- Do not post identical promotional messages in many groups. Follow each group's rules.
- Do not publish unapproved photos, staff faces, customers' homes, other brands' logos or client names.
- Do not copy another business's photos, text or videos.
- Do not promise prices, times or results that the owner has not confirmed.
- Do not add neighbourhoods to profiles, posts or ads until the owner verifies them.
- Do not publish the registered business address (a residential unit).

## Risks and open items

- The pricing scope, photo consent, vector logo, favicon, "Since 2023", the "Amirul" label and
  neighbourhoods are still open ([README.md](README.md)). The work above avoids relying on them.
- The production URL and analytics decision come first. Without them, campaign links cannot be
  finalised and website visits cannot be counted.
- Results depend on AJ's capacity and response speed as much as on marketing.
